01 — About Me

A student of code,
a wanderer of words.

I'm a freshman at Hangzhou Dianzi University, studying Artificial Intelligence. While my days are filled with algorithms, data structures, and the logic of machines, my nights belong to novels, poetry, and the endless curiosity about what it means to be human.

This summer, I traveled with a camera and a notebook. I believe that every place has a story to tell, and every photograph is a sentence in a larger narrative. This site began as a simple card for my English teacher — a way to say "hello" beyond the classroom — but I hope it grows into something more: a small space for writing, images, and the feelings that connect them.
